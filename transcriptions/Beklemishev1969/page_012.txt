[RUNNING HEAD] DEVELOPMENT OF THE COLONIAL HABIT	457

Typically colonies of Phylactolaemata are formed of pseudomonopodial shoots that radiate over the substrate, with the apical ends of the separate zooids bent away from the substrate (Plumatella, Fig. 208, A; Fredericella). Crust-like colonies of these species are formed by adherence and fusion of the spreading shoots.
