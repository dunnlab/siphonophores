Fig. 211. Structure of Stenolaemata colonies.

A—beginning of formation of Berenicea patina colony: young colony (in this species, of incrustational type), view from basal side. 1—round original disc (primary individual); 2-4—zooids (numbered in order of age); 5—common colonial growth zone not divided by septa. B—Diplosolen obelia, part of colony (incrustational type) with gonozooid. Besides autozooids there are numerous small nanozooids. The gonozooid is of irregular shape; its upper wall is broken to show its narrow proximal part (1), which does not differ from the proximal parts of the autozooids; 2—the distal, tubular part of the gonozooid, with aperture.
