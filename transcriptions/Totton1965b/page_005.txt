[RUNNING HEAD] A. K. Totton : on three species of Lensia (Siphonophora)	75

Anterior nectophore: (fig. 3 A, B, C). Resembling those of L. achilles and L. baryi, but with a squat, heart-shaped somatocyst situated above the ostial level. The longitudinal ridges bear well marked crests, unlike those of L. baryi. Length ≃10·5 mm.

Posterior nectophores unknown.

Eudoxid phase unknown.
