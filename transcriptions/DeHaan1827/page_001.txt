[NOTE: Title page of the volume. Dutch, letterpress on laid paper, from a low-resolution scan; the type is large and legible but the library stamp is not. "BIJDRAGEN" is set in a large decorative outlined-and-shadowed titling face; the remaining lines are in plain roman capitals and small capitals, with the imprint line in italic. Foxing and a few brown stains cross the page, heaviest at the left edge and around the middle. The line "TWEEDE DEEL , EERSTE STUK." is printed with a space before the comma, in the Continental manner, and sits between two pairs of double rules. Between the rules and the imprint there is a typographic ornament (a lozenge-and-bead fleuron); a second, smaller ornament separates the imprint from the year. A handwritten pencil "2" sits below the double rules, centred. The oval library stamp at the foot is a blind-and-ink device with a coat of arms in the centre and lettering running round the rim, but at this resolution not one letter of it can be read.]
