[NOTE: the whole page below the running head is one full-page plate of four photomicrographs in two rows, with the shared caption printed beneath. The only lettering on the plate is the four figure numbers, set in bold in the outer margins beside each photograph: 6 at the left and 7 at the right of the upper row, 8 at the left and 9 at the right of the lower row; they are transcribed above in reading order, the two rows on separate lines. The photographs themselves carry no labels, letters or scale bars. In the caption the species name is printed "Muggiaea", with an unmistakable e (crossbar clearly visible at high magnification), unlike the "Muggiaca" of the caption to Figg. 2-5 on p. 65. The separator after "Figg. 6-9." is a short hyphen-length dash, the same width as the hyphen in "6-9", not an em dash. A pale bleached/abraded patch in the caption line damages the "i" of the second "di" and the first two letters of "gonofori": the "i" is reduced to its dot but is legible, while "go" is largely gone, leaving "nofori" clear — hence the flagged reading. A stray speck sits between "nel" and "testo". Faint dashed rules and scattered specks and blots run around all four sides of the plate area (printer's crop or registration marks and dirt) and are not type. Only the lower halves of the two folio digits at the top right fall inside the scanned area; what remains — a round bowl followed by a narrow tapering stroke — is consistent with 67, the expected folio between 66 and 68.]
