5.	The transformation of the siphonula into a young colony is characterised by the completion of the pneumatophore which differentiates the aeriform tissue and the hollowing of several cavities; the differentiation of the primary gastrozooid which acquires three types of glandular endodermic cells defined by Semal–Van Gansen; the budding, between the pneumatophore and the primary gastrozooid, of some first nectophores and some cormidia

This study has allowed me to find, in the formation of the pneumatophore, the same stages that have been described for Forskalia (Carré, D. 1967). In addition, I have been led to dispute the ideas of a true morula, of secondary delamination and of a primary endoderm, but I cannot bring to this study, based on a single species, any general conclusions on the first stages of development of siphonophores.
