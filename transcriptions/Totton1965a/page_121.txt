Polygastric phase

(Text-fig. 65.) Three categories of nectophores are known: (1) caducous, larval, up to 12 mm in length; (2) definitive nectophore number 1, up to 21 mm in length; (3) definitive nectophore number 2, up to 32 mm in length; they differ in shape from one another. This is probably true of R. cymbiformis too, but the succession has not been described in that species.
