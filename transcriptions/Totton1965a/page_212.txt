Polygastric phase

Anterior nectophore:   The chief diagnostic feature cited by Sears is that the hydroecium extends to about one-half the overall length instead of one-third as in C. sagittata, so that the apical prolongation is relatively shorter than in C. sagittata, though longer than in C. dentata.

Posterior nectophore and gonophore:   Unknown.

Eudoxid phase – not identified.
