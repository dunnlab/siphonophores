[NOTE: this leaf is entirely occupied by PLATE VIII, a single reproduction of Lesueur's engraving filling almost the whole leaf. Its caption is on the facing leaf, PDF page 249; no caption text is repeated here.]

[NOTE: "PLATE VIII" is set in bold full capitals above the top right corner of the block, outside the image area, in the same modern fount as the other plate numbers in this volume.]

[NOTE: TWO DISTINCT KINDS OF LETTERING are present on this plate, and the difference matters. (a) The original engraved labels, in a small 19th-century roman with a full point after the F: "F. 1." at the top right, "F.5" at the centre right, "F.6" at the centre, "F.7", "F.8" and "F.9" across the middle, "F.11" at the left, "F.12" and "F.13" at the lower left, and "F.14." at the lower right. (b) Four figure numbers in a much larger and heavier modern face, evidently added for this reproduction and not engraved by Lesueur: "2", "3", "4" and "10". There is no "F.2", "F.3", "F.4" or "F.10" in the engraved series, and no label of either kind for figures numbered 1 and 5 through 14 beyond those listed. The tokens are listed above in reading order, top-left to bottom-right, with the engraved and modern labels interleaved as they physically fall.]

[NOTE: besides the figure labels the plate carries small engraved italic reference letters within the figures. Around the group labelled 10 there are four separate italic "a"s (one beside the upper crescent-shaped object, one at the left of the row below, one below the central object, one at the right of the row). At F.5 there are an italic "a" above right, an italic "e" below left and an italic "d" further below left. At F.11 there are two italic "a"s down the left side and two italic "b"s down the centre-right. All eleven are listed above in the [PLATE] block in their reading positions.]

[NOTE: the engraver's signature is engraved in fine cursive script along the foot of the plate, left of centre, below all the figures. UNCERTAIN READING: it reads "Le Sueur Del[?t]. & Sculp." — the abbreviation between "Del" and the point carries a dot above a short ascender and is equally readable as "Delt." (delineavit) or "Deli."; the letter itself cannot be resolved at this resolution. "Le Sueur" is written as two words in the script, unlike the "Lesueur" of the printed caption on PDF page 249. The square brackets in "Del[?t]." are the transcriber's, not part of the engraving. This is the one token on the leaf that would benefit from a second opinion.]

[NOTE: there is no printed folio, no running head and no signature mark of the printer on this leaf.]

[NOTE: no printed square brackets appear on this leaf.]

[NOTE: the top left corner of the reproduction shows the edges of two lighter rectangular patches behind figures 2, 3, 4 and 10 — the join lines of separate pieces of the original engraving mounted together for photography. They carry no lettering.]
