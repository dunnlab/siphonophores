174	[RUNNING HEAD] A SYNOPSIS OF THE SIPHONOPHORA

Lensia fowleri (Bigelow, 1911)
Text-figures 112, 113

Diphyes fowleri Bigelow, 1911a

For a good figure see Bigelow, 1911a, plate 28, figure 5.

A common five-ridged form whose globular somatocyst lies below the level of the ostium; first described from the Bay of Biscay, and taken in the Mediterranean (Bigelow & Sears, 1937, p. 139) at the rate of 250 per cu. metre of water. It resembles in general both L. hardy and L. challengeri, but is distinguished at once by the position of the somatocyst.
