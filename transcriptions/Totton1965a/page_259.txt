[NOTE: PRINTED QUERY MARK, NOT A TRANSCRIBER MARKER. The second species heading begins with a bare printed question mark set close up against the italic generic name — "?Moseria convoluta (Moser)" — the author's own editorial query as to the identification. It is NOT enclosed in square brackets, unlike the "[? Prayidae]" form used elsewhere in this monograph, and it is not one of my markers. No printed square brackets occur anywhere on this leaf.]

[NOTE: PRINTER'S ERROR PRESERVED. FIG. 2 reads "Tentillum, = 7." with a printed EQUALS sign where every other magnification on the leaf has a multiplication sign. At magnification the mark is unmistakably two short parallel horizontal strokes, not a crossed ×. Transcribed as printed.]

[NOTE: FIG. 4 runs to two printed lines, the second indented to align under "Part"; they are joined into one line above. No line-break hyphen is involved.]

[NOTE: "'Discovery'" is printed in single quotation marks — a turned comma opening and an apostrophe closing — for the ship's name; it is roman, not italic. The station data print a degree sign and a prime for minutes: "47° 46' S, 00° 28.6' E". The longitude is printed with two zeros, "00°". The depth range "1500-1000 m" is printed high-to-low in that order, with an en dash, and is transcribed as printed.]

[NOTE: "FIG."/"FIGS." in each caption line is printed with a full-size F and small-capital following letters and is transcribed in full capitals. The tabs above stand for the printed spaces. The multiplication signs are printed multiplication signs, not the letter x. "116-118", "5-8" and "1500-1000" are printed with en dashes; "9, 10" with a comma.]

[NOTE: all numerals on the leaf are printed in old-style (text) figures, so that 1 has no upper serif, 2 sits on the baseline with a descending tail in "2023", and 0 is small and round; transcribed as ordinary digits. The plate number and the source plate number are roman, "XIII" and "XVI".]
