There are twenty-one described species. Some species have no crested ridges, some have three, some five, some seven, some are multicrestate, and in one there is a ridged reticulation of the surface. The multicrestate species probably do not form a distinct sub-group because one of them, lelouveteau, like the reticulate one reticulata appear to be related to meteori and subtilis neither of which are ridged.
