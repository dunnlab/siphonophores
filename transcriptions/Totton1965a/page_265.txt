[NOTE: the key on this leaf is NOT the usual italic lower-case a, b, c list. It is a running prose sentence introducing German abbreviations in italic CAPITALS — SK, DK, VK¹, VK², UK, TK, KF, Z, DF, VG — each followed by a printed "=" sign and an English gloss, the glosses separated by semicolons. "VK¹" and "VK²" are printed with true SUPERSCRIPT arabic numerals 1 and 2 following the italic capitals, transcribed above with the Unicode superscripts ¹ and ². The sentence opens "All the following terms, except the last, are translations of those used by Claus:" — the exception being VG, the last entry, which is Totton's own. There is no key at all for figures 8-10.]

[NOTE: several of the printed equals signs in the key paragraph are inked unevenly in this scan, the two strokes of unequal length or the upper stroke broken — notably in "DK =", "Z =" and "DF =" — but all ten are equals signs. The three magnifications in figures 8, 9 and 10 are printed with proper multiplication signs, ×, not with equals signs.]

[NOTE: "FIG." in every entry is printed with a full-size F and small-capital IG followed by a full point, and is transcribed in full capitals. Only FIG. 8 runs to two printed lines; its second line begins "phore and nectophore)", completing "pneumato-" broken at the end of the first line, and the line-break hyphen has been silently removed above to give "pneumatophore".]

[NOTE: "Claus's" is printed with an apostrophe and a second s, and occurs four times, in the descriptions to figures 3, 4, 6 and 7.
