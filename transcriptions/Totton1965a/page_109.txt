Gastrozooids:   Forty to fifty on a 20 cm long specimen (contracted); from 10 to 19 mm in length. They are very mobile and are carried on long peduncles which measure 12 mm or more in length. There are from eight to twelve rose-red 'liver stripes' within the stomach. The cnido-band of the tentillum of the tentacle is fiery red in colour, with no involucre and is spirally twisted into two to four turns. On either side of the cnidoband is a lateral row of bean-shaped nematocysts, 0·51 mm long and 0·16 mm broad. The long terminal filament carries short bean-shaped nematocysts, 0·003 mm in length. There are two pairs of folded cords in the tentillum, very much better developed according to Leuckart than in Halistemma rubrum.
