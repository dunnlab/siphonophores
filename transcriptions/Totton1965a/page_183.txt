178	[RUNNING HEAD] A SYNOPSIS OF THE SIPHONOPHORA

Lensia grimaldi Leloup, 1933
Text-figure 117

L. grimaldi Totton, 1941

A midwater form.

Polygastric phase

Anterior nectophore:   Multistriate with a partial (dorsal region) ostial ridge. There is a dorsal ridge (or group) with a (sometimes a third incomplete one present) pair of longitudinal crests (see Totton, 1941, fig. 29); pair of lateral ridges (or group) with a pair of crests on each; and a ventral group of four crests. The bases of the dorso-lateral ridges run down dorsad to near the mid-dorsal point of the ostial margin. Just short of this point they join an incomplete (basal part of) dorsal ridge. The hydroecium is deep, not rising much above the ostial level, with a well rounded ventro-basal margin, and open ventrally. The somatocyst is short and club-shaped.

Posterior nectophore:   Unknown.

Eudoxid phase – not identified.
