[NOTE: the figures are NOT laid out in numerical order. Read left to right and top to bottom, the arrangement is: a top band of 1. (a tall figure occupying the whole left edge of the plate), 2., 4., 11. (a second tall figure running the full height of the plate at centre-right) and 13.; then 3., 14. and 16.; then 15. and 12.; then 8., 7. and 17.; then 9. and 10.; and finally 5. and 6. along the bottom left. Figures 3., 5. and 8. carry a number but no reference letters, and are listed with their number alone.]

[NOTE: every reference letter is engraved in small italic and joined to the structure it names by a fine dotted or ruled leader. Sixty-two reference letters in all across the seventeen figures.]

[NOTE: two reference letters on this plate do not appear in the key printed on the facing caption leaf (PDF page 253), which lists lc but no lo, and cc but no ca. (a) Figure 4 carries "lo": at magnification the second glyph is an unmistakably closed round bowl with no ascender, quite unlike the open italic c used in "cc" on the same figure, so "lo" is what is engraved. (b) Figure 13's lower-left letter is transcribed "[?ca]": the first glyph is a clear open italic c, but the second is a closed bowl with a short right-hand stroke, matching the "a" of "amp" on figure 17 rather than the open c of "cc" on figures 2, 4 and 12. The alternative reading is "cc"; the leader line meets the letter at exactly that point and may be closing an open c. 300 dpi is the limit of the scan here and the two readings cannot be separated with confidence. SECOND OPINION WANTED on this one token.]

[NOTE: the peduncular-lamellae letter is engraved on figure 16 as "st br", in two parts with a clear word space and NO full point, twice. The key on the facing caption leaf prints it "st.br" with an internal full point. Transcribed as engraved on the plate.]

[NOTE: the terminal-filament letter on figure 9 is engraved "t.f" with an internal full point, agreeing with the key.]
