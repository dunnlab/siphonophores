Lensia achilles Totton, 1941
Text-figure 106 (see p. 170)

A five-ridged, deep-water form, coloured deep or bright orange in life.

Polygastric phase

Anterior nectophore (text-fig. 106):   Five complete ridges, the basal ends the of laterals bending dorsad. Vault of hydroecium level with the ostium, with a deep notch on the baso-ventral margin. Somatocysts spindle-shaped. The nectophore shown by Bigelow (1913, fig. 2) almost certainly belongs to this species, but his descriptions of the two nectophores may refer to more than one species.

Posterior nectophore:   The nectophore shown by Bigelow (1913, Pl. 6, figs. 3, 4) may belong to this species.

Eudoxid phase – undescribed.
