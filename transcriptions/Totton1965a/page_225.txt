Eudoxid phase (Pl. XL, fig. 5)

Bract:   There is a long baso-sagittal ridge as in Abylopsis eschscholtzi but an apical ridge replaces an apical facet.

There are no lateral branches of the phyllocyst, but there is a large apical and a long narrow basal branch.

Genus: ENNEAGONUM Quoy & Gaimard, 1827

Cuboides Q. & G., 1827
Cuboides Bigelow, 1911b

Monotypic genus for E. hyalinum Q. & G., 1827.

If a suitable specimen of E. hyalinum – there is some variation – is orientated as in my (1932) text-figure 16 it will be seen that its features are homologous with those of Abylopsis and Bassia spp., except for the presence of a dorsal ridge. This was emphasized by Sears (1953), having been previously referred to by Huxley (1859) and Totton (1932). No posterior nectophore is produced.
