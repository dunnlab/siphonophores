FIG. 74. Bracts of Prayidae
Some bracts of eudoxids to show how the branches of the bracteal canals may be homologized with one another:
A, Nectopyramis thetis Bigelow (after Totton, 1954, fig. 36B), × 5.5.
B, N. diomedeae Bigelow, full grown bract (after, Bigelow, 1911b, Pl. 1, fig. 5), × 3.
C, N. diomedeae Bigelow, juvenile bract (after Bigelow, 1911b, fig. A, p. 193), × 40.
D, N. spinosa Sears, × 11.
E, N. natans (Bigelow) (after Bigelow, 1911b, Pl. 20, fig. 6), × 3.
F, Rosacea cymbiformis (Chiaje) (after Bigelow, 1911b, Pl. 2, fig. 4), × 15.
G, Praya reticulata (Bigelow) (after Bigelow, 1911b, Pl. 3, fig. 6), × 3.
H, P. dubia (Q. & G.), × 3.
K, Amphicaryon acaule Chun (after Bigelow, 1911b, Pl. 4, fig. 5), × 50.
L, Maresearsia praeclara Totton (after Totton, 1954, fig. 48A), × 9.
c.h = hydroecial canal; c1 = spur canal; c3 = dorsal canal; c4 = ventral canal.
[/FIGURE]
