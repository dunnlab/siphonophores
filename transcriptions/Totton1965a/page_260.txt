[NOTE: this leaf is entirely occupied by PLATE XIII. Its caption is on the facing leaf, PDF page 259; no caption text is repeated here. The plate is printed portrait on a portrait leaf and needs no rotation; every figure number reads upright.]

[NOTE: "PLATE XIII" is set in bold full capitals in the top right corner of the leaf, above and clear of the plate, in the ordinary text orientation. It is the only text on the leaf outside the blocks.]

[NOTE: THE FIGURE NUMBERS ARE THE ONLY TEXT ON THIS PLATE. There are no reference letters of any kind — not one lettering token, on any of the ten figures. The whole plate block was swept quadrant by quadrant at magnification to confirm this. The numerals are printed in a heavy sans-serif face WITHOUT a following full point; the full points shown in the [PLATE] block above are mine, added only to separate the entries, and should be read as "1", "4", "3", "2", "5" … "10".]

[NOTE: the plate is assembled from seven separate blocks laid out in a grid two blocks wide and four rows deep. The top row is a line block on the left carrying figures 1, 3 and 2 together, and a half-tone photographic block on the right carrying figure 4. The three rows below are all half-tone photographic blocks, one figure each: 5 left and 6 right; 7 left and 8 right; 9 left and 10 right. Figure 5's block contains three separate objects and figure 7's block five, but each block carries only its own single number.]

[NOTE: the figure numbers are NOT in layout order. Within the top-left line block, "1" stands at the top left, "3" below it at the lower left, and "2" to the right of "3" at mid-height — so the numerical order 1, 2, 3 runs top-left, then right, then lower-left. The [PLATE] block above lists the numbers in reading order down and across the leaf: 1 and 4 in the top row, then 3 and 2 lower in the top-left block, then 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 row by row.]

[NOTE: figure 4's numeral is reversed out WHITE against the dark background of its photograph, at the block's upper left. All the other numerals are printed solid black — figures 1, 2 and 3 on the white of the line block, figures 5-10 on the light grey of their photographs.]

[NOTE: two overlapping solid dark rectangles are printed across the middle right of figure 4's photograph, one larger and one smaller, masking part of the subject. They carry no text and are part of the block as printed — retouching or masking in the original photograph, not scanner damage.]

[NOTE: several long fine straight lines cross the specimens in figures 5 and 7 — two nearly horizontal in figure 5, one horizontal and one diagonal in figure 7. They are mounting wires or scratches in the photographic blocks, not rules or leaders, and carry no lettering.]

[NOTE: there is no printed folio, no running head and no printer's signature mark on this leaf. Isolated grey specks occur in the white margins around the blocks, including a small one low in the left margin and two in the right margin.]

[NOTE: no printed square brackets and no editorial query marks appear on this leaf. The only square brackets above are transcriber markers.]
