[NOTE: the plate number "PLATE III" is engraved in bold full capitals above the top right corner of the block group, outside the images. All other printed characters are lettering set into the blocks, reversed out in white or printed in black with a fine leader rule to the structure indicated. They are listed above block by block, and within each block top to bottom, with a blank line between blocks. The block numbers are NOT in the usual reading order: "1" is in the upper left block, "3" in the upper right, "2" in the lower left and "4" in the lower right, so the numbering runs down the left column and then down the right.]

[NOTE: block 1 (upper left) carries, after its number: "St", "T", "Gz", "Go", then lower down a second "T" and a second "Gz". Block 3 (upper right) carries only its number and one "St" at the right, about two-thirds down. Block 2 (lower left) carries "Gz" upper right, "Go" at centre left, a second "Gz" lower right, "St" lower left and a second "Go" at the foot. Block 4 (lower right) carries "P" upper right, a second "P" at the left, "G.b", a third "P", "N", a second "G.b", a second "N", a third "G.b" and a fourth "G.b" descending the right side. Counts on this plate: seven tokens in block 1 including the number, two in block 3, six in block 2, ten in block 4.]
