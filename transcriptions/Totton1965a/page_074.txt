FIG. 34.   Nanomia bijuga (Chiaje)
Four views of full grown nectophores (Villefranche specimens) of the auriculate variety.
A, fronto-lateral view; B, lateral view; C, view from above; D, ostio-superior view. Nectophore A is from
one specimen (B.M. Reg. No. 1953.7.2.45); B–D from another (B.M. Reg. No. 1953.9.23.604)
a–d = junctions of ridges. Junctions a, b, d, found only in auriculate varieties; p.w = wedge process;
r.l = lateral ridge; vk1, vk2 = vertical ridges named by Claus (1878).
