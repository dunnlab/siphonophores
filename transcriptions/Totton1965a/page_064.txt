Halistemma striata sp.n.
Text-figure 24

The species is known only by very distinctive nectophores, which vary in width from 8 mm to 19 mm. The figured specimen is selected as the type. It was taken off Bermuda by W. Beebe.

Nectophore:   There is a characteristic arrangement of four lateral ridges (text-fig. 24, r, r, r), which descend from the upper lateral ridge (vk' of Claus; see text-fig. 34) at an angle of about 30° from the vertical towards the ostial end of the base.
