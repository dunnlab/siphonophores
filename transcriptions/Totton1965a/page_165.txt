A, anterior nectophore, × 22; B, posterior nectophore, × 32; C, ventral view of the proximal end of the posterior nectophore, × 23. hy.t = tooth at the apex of the right hydroecial fold (from Totton, 1932, fig. 32).
[/FIGURE]

12·5 m depth, especially from December to the middle of February, in water of comparatively low salinity inside the Barrier Reef.

Polygastric phase

Anterior nectophore (see Lens & van Riemsdijk, 1908, Pl. VII, fig. 59; and text-fig. 95):   Up to 5 mm in length and 2 mm deep, five ridged with shallow hydroecium and an oblique ovoid stalked somatocyst about 1·2 mm in length, the ovoid part varying from 0·54 to 0·66 mm in length, and from 0·23 to 0·28 mm in diameter. The loop of the lateral canals reaches within a distance of 0·20 mm from the anterior end of the nectosac and of 0·37 mm of the apex of the nectophore. The two halves of the mouth-plate are of almost equal size, rounded and slightly projecting below the base. The anterior wall of the hydroecium extends forward to the level of the ostium. The basal end of the dorsal ridge does not form a marked tooth-like projection.

Posterior nectophore:   There is no projection of the nectosac anterior to the entry of the pedicular canal. There is a small tooth at the apex of the right hydroecial fold.

Eudoxid phase

(Text-fig. 96.)   There are no projections on the posterior margin of the bract. The phyllocyst is club-shaped.
