Sub-family ii: PRAYINAE Chun, 1897

In my view there are six groups of prayine species; (A) two well-known species with simple somatocysts or pallial canals and a pair of nectophores (with simple radial sub-umbral canals, the laterals meandering). In their polygastric and eudoxid phases they resemble each other more than either does other species, and so may be grouped under one generic name Rosacea (sensu Bigelow, 1911b).
