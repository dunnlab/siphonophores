There is a succession of buds. I have seen a pair, a larger posterior with a smaller anterior, as shown by Bigelow (1918, fig. 198). There was in addition a third rudiment on the base of the pedicel of the small anterior bud.

Tentilla:   Of the basic calycophoran pattern. The pedicel is folded concertina-wise in its distal part, next to the saccus. In the saccus it is continued by a single 'Angelband', also folded concertina-wise, larger in diameter distally where it is attached to the outer end of the saccus, at the junction of cnidoband, rowel (see 3 below) and terminal filament. There are three chief types of nematocysts, apart from those of the terminal filament:
