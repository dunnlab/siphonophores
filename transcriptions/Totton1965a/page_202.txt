Crystallophyes amygdalina Moser, 1925
Text-figures 133, 134

Described originally from three nectophores found off the Antarctic continent. Reported again by Leloup (1934) (two specimens from S. Georgia) and Totton (1954, seventy-two anterior and twenty-three associated posterior nectophores). It is evidently a mid-water form.
