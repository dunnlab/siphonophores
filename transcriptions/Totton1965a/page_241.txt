[RUNNING HEAD] A SYNOPSIS OF THE SIPHONOPHORA

PLATE IV	Rhizophysa filiformis (Forskål)

Photographs of living specimens from Villefranche, 1963

FIG. 1.	Two pieces of one tentacle. Below (left), some 4 cm from the proximal end showing early buds of tentilla into which some of the nematocysts have entered from the ectoderm. The longitudinal muscle band can be seen on the lower side. Above (right), a piece of tentacle some 2 cm nearer the proximal end. Only one tentillum has been entered by a single nematocyst.
FIG. 2.	A tricornuate tentillum showing the web between the branches.
FIG. 3.	View of a tentacle focused on two dendritic tentilla, partly expanded.
FIG. 4.	A bird-headed or beaked tentillum.
FIG. 5.	Partly expanded tricornuate and dendritic tentilla.
FIG. 6.	Three fully expanded dendritic tentilla showing opaque areas.
